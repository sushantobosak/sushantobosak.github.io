About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. My research focuses on control-oriented modeling and nonlinear control of power-electronic energy systems, particularly grid-integrated hydrogen electrolyzers, energy storage, and ancillary service applications.

I completed my M.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from RUET with a CGPA of 4.00/4.00 and my B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from RUET with a CGPA of 3.96/4.00, ranking 1st among 123 students.

Research Interests

Grid-integrated hydrogen electrolyzers and fuel cell systems
Control-oriented modeling and nonlinear control of power-electronic systems
Energy storage systems and hybrid energy architectures
Optimization-based and data-informed control methods for power and energy systems
